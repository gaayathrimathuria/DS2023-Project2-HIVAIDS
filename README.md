# DS2023 Project 2

## Project Overview 

For our project topic, we decided to look into HIV and AIDS data from California between 2010 to 2023. Our dataset was obtained from the California Department of Public Health and the three CSV's we will be using are linked here. This project falls within the domains of public health, epidemiology, and health data analytics. It uses California HIV and AIDS case data to investigate patterns in disease incidence and how those patterns vary across populations and over time. 

## Research Questions (Task, Sub-Task, and Operation Level) 

1. How prevalent have HIV and AIDS been in California over time (since 2010)? 
   1. What is the relationship between the number of new cases and time? 
      1. What is the total number of new diagnoses each year? 
   2. What is the relationship between the number of existing cases and time? 
      1. What is the total number of persons living with HIV/AIDS in each year? 
   3. Do certain districts have greater numbers of new and existing cases? 
      1. What are the total new and living cases for each county across 2010-2023? 

2. Does HIV (and AIDS) have a greater impact on certain demographics (age, race, gender, etc.) than others? 
   1. Are there any demographics that have consistently higher counts of new and existing cases across the several years? 
      1. What are the new and living counts for each group within age, race/ethnicity, and current gender each year? 
   2. Are certain transmission methods more prevalent among certain demographics? 
      1. What is the count for each transmission method within each gender category as well as for children?
   
3. Do certain demographics have a higher risk of dying from HIV and/or AIDS? 
      1. Do certain demographics have higher death counts across several years? 
         1. What is the death count for each group within age, race/ethnicity, and current gender in each year? 
      2. Do certain demographics have higher death counts relative to overall cases within that demographic across several years? 
         1. What is the ratio of deaths to living cases for each group in each year? 

4. How have deaths from HIV and AIDS in California changed over time (since 2010)? 
   1. Have deaths increased or decreased each year? 
      1. What is the total number of deaths in each year? 
   2. How have deaths changed relative to the number of overall cases? 
      1. What is the ratio of deaths to new and living cases each year?
